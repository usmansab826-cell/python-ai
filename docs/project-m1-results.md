# Module 1 Project: AI Test Results

**Business:** Y-Computers, Lahore

**Model used for the recorded examples:** gemma4:31b-cloud

These are the generated examples that were reviewed. A useful-sounding answer
is not automatically safe to publish: business claims, names, prices, and
promises still need human verification.

## 1. Marketing

**Prompt:** The Prompt Kit requested a friendly Instagram post for Y-Computers
in Lahore, aimed at the stated customers, with a call to action.

**Result summary:** The answer included a friendly tone and a call to action,
but it also introduced unsupported details such as SRDW, DV360 services, and
expert IT support.

**Assessment:** Partially useful as a draft. Remove or verify every service and
expertise claim before publishing. The prompt should be updated to explicitly
say not to add services that were not provided.

## 2. Customer service

**Prompt:** The Prompt Kit requested an apology for a late order and a small
discount without specifying discount terms.

**Result summary:** The reply was polite and did not specify a discount amount,
but it invented a customer name, a manager name, company performance claims,
and a promise that corrective steps were being taken.

**Assessment:** Useful only after editing. Replace invented names with
placeholders, remove unverified company claims, and have an authorized person
approve any discount commitment.

## 3. Growth ideas

**Prompt:** Suggest three low-cost AI ideas within a monthly budget of
200,000 PKR and label estimates.

**Result summary:** The answer listed estimates of 10,000–25,000 PKR,
55,000–95,000 PKR, and 25,000–45,000 PKR. Those ranges total 90,000–165,000
PKR, but the answer claimed 140,000–250,000 PKR.

**Assessment:** Not ready to use. Its stated upper total exceeds the budget,
and the arithmetic is inconsistent. Verify estimates and recalculate totals.

## Overall result

The prompt builder produces three relevant prompt types, but the generated
outputs still need human review. Next improvement: include verified business
facts in each prompt and explicitly prohibit invented names, services, prices,
and commitments.
